Cascade Viewer is a self-hosted gallery for local creator archives. Justified grid, full-screen lightbox, search by post text, date navigation and on-demand video thumbs — all in one Docker image.

No cloud.
No tracking.
Browse thousands of posts from your local `*-of` archives in the browser.
