Cascade Viewer is a self-hosted gallery built for local OnlyFans archives. Justified grid, full-screen lightbox, search by post text, date navigation and on-demand video thumbs — all in one Docker image.

No cloud.
No tracking.
Browse thousands of posts and media from your local `*-of` archives in the browser.

Works with archives produced by the companion OF sync sidecar (`metadata.json` + `Timeline` / `Messages` / `Stories`).

  viewer:
    image: ghcr.io/manawenuz/cascade-viewer:latest
    ports: ["3102:3102"]
    environment:
      OF_BASE: /data
      OF_THUMBS: /thumbs
    volumes:
      - /mnt/pool1/of-downloads:/data:ro
      - /mnt/pool1/thumbs:/thumbs
[/code]
