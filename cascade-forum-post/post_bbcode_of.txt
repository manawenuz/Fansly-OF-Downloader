[size=26]
Cascade Viewer is a self-hosted gallery built for local OnlyFans archives. Justified grid, full-screen lightbox, search by post text, date navigation and on-demand video thumbs — all in one Docker image.

No cloud.
No tracking.
Browse thousands of posts and media from your local `*-of` archives in the browser.

Works with archives produced by the companion OF sync sidecar (`metadata.json` + `Timeline` / `Messages` / `Stories`).

[size=30][b]📂 FOLDER STRUCTURE[/b][/size]

[size=26]
Viewer reads the archive layout produced by the OF sync sidecar:
[/size]

[code]
/data/
  cherrylovebombb-of/
    metadata.json
    Timeline/
    Messages/
    Stories/
  thelegogoddess-of/
    metadata.json
    Timeline/
[/code]

[list]
[*][size=26][b]metadata.json[/b] – OnlyFans posts index, cached by mtime[/size]
[*][size=26][b]Timeline / Messages / Stories[/b] – media folders, served as-is[/size]
[*][size=26][b].viewer-thumbs[/b] – generated video thumbs (480px, separate volume)[/size]
[/list]

[hr]

[size=30][b]🐳 DOCKER QUICK START[/b][/size]

[code]
services:
  viewer:
    image: ghcr.io/manawenuz/cascade-viewer:latest
    container_name: cascade-viewer
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports: ["3102:3102"]
    environment:
      OF_BASE: /data
      OF_THUMBS: /thumbs
    volumes:
      - /mnt/pool1/of-downloads:/data:ro
      - /mnt/pool1/thumbs:/thumbs
[/code]

[size=26]
Open http://localhost:3102 — creators appear automatically from `*-of` folders.
[/size]
